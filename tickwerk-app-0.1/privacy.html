Microphone

Tickwerk needs the microphone to hear your watch ticking. It is only open while a measurement is running and is closed immediately afterwards. The recording stays in the phone’s working memory, is analyzed there and is not saved. It only leaves the phone if you pass it on to another app yourself with “Share recording”.

In regulate mode, Tickwerk only keeps the last few seconds in working memory and saves nothing. If you search for microphones under “Settings”, Tickwerk asks the browser for their list; the names stay on the phone and only your choice is saved.

What is stored on the phone

Your watches, the measurements (rate, beat error, amplitude, beat rate, position, date, signal quality, a reduced paper tape), your time checks for the rate on the wrist and the settings are kept in the app’s storage on your phone. Nobody but you has access to them, not even us.

You can delete everything under More → Settings → “Delete all data”. The data is kept in the storage that Chrome keeps for Tickwerk. If you uninstall the app, it stays there until you delete Tickwerk’s site data in Chrome. Conversely, if you delete all site data in Chrome, your watches and measurements are gone too. A backup protects you against that.

Tickwerk creates a backup (“Save backup”), the measurement report (PDF), an image of a result (“Share as image”) and a calendar file (.ics) on your phone. What happens to these files afterwards is up to you.

Calendar reminders

If you tap “Google Calendar” for a reminder, Google Calendar opens with a prepared event. The title (with the name of the watch), the time and a short text are sent to Google, under Google’s terms and privacy policy. Nothing is sent to Google unless you tap. Tickwerk creates the “calendar file” on the phone, without the internet.

No ads, no analytics

Tickwerk uses no cookies, no advertising or analytics services and no fonts or scripts from third-party servers. There is no user account.

Loading the app

The app’s files are hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When the app is loaded or updated, this provider processes technically necessary data such as IP address, time and browser type in order to deliver the files and prevent misuse. After that, Tickwerk also works without internet.
